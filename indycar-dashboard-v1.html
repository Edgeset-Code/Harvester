Why ICPs matter for INDYCAR direct marketing

Each ICP below is a deployable audience segment derived from real engagement patterns in INDYCAR's social channels. INDYCAR's marketing team can use these to:
  • Time race promo — target NASCAR fans for Watkins Glen, European purists for Le Mans broadcast, etc.
  • Tailor messaging — manufacturer rivalry for American loyalists, technical depth for European purists
  • Segment email — distinct flows per cluster instead of batch-and-blast
  • Build lookalikes — each ICP becomes a seed for Meta / Google custom audiences (Phase 2)
  • Bridge to sponsors — each card shows brand affinity, identifying overlap with sponsor prospects

⊞ Positioning matrix · premium/mass × global/domestic

Where each series sits on the two axes that matter for sponsor conversations. Bubble size = total score.
The read: F1 owns premium/global. NASCAR owns mass/domestic. WEC and IMSA both anchor endurance — WEC global, IMSA domestic with an ACO/Le Mans bridge. IndyCar sits mid, strategically ambiguous — and that ambiguity is IndyCar's biggest constraint.
