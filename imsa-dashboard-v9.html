Why ICPs matter for IMSA direct marketing

Each ICP below is a deployable audience segment derived from real engagement patterns in IMSA's social channels. IMSA's marketing team can use these to:
  • Time race promo — target NASCAR fans for Watkins Glen, European purists for Le Mans broadcast, etc.
  • Tailor messaging — manufacturer rivalry for American loyalists, technical depth for European purists
  • Segment email — distinct flows per cluster instead of batch-and-blast
  • Build lookalikes — each ICP becomes a seed for Meta / Google custom audiences (Phase 2)
  • Bridge to sponsors — each card shows brand affinity, identifying overlap with sponsor prospects
