Pre-race conversation timing

Engagement pattern around race weekends: pre-race build, peak weekend, post-race tail. Real timing data from Laguna Seca and Watkins Glen.
3-4 days pre-race: +20% engagement vs baseline
Race day: 4× baseline — peak volume window
Day +1 (next day): 60% of race-day volume — analysis posts
Day +3 (tail): +10% vs baseline — discussion fades
Marketing actionable: the 3-4 days pre-race is the highest-leverage promo window — engagement is rising but conversation isn't crowded yet. Race-day social spend is competing for attention.
